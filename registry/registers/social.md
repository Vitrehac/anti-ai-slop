# Social post register

**When:** LinkedIn, X/Twitter threads (plain text), job-hunt posts, outreach DMs pasted as text.

**Not for:** intentional cringe (`linktwerk`). Route those separately.

## Voice

- One person, one situation, one point.
- Numbers and names when true; never invent stats or survey citations.
- OK to ask for work or a reply; not OK to beg engagement.

## Hard bans (social-specific)

- Fake stats ("A 2024 Stack Overflow survey found…") unless user provides a real link
- Hashtag walls (5+ tags on one line); max 2–3 relevant tags or none
- Punch-pause lines: "Let that sink in.", "Chew on that.", "Read that again."
- Repost/tag/comment bait: "Repost if…", "Tag someone who…", "Drop one sentence…"
- Linktwerk residue: "Operating manual updates", "Signals I ignored", arrow-list life lessons
- Staccato poetry (4+ consecutive ultra-short lines for drama)
- Lesson lists after a personal story ("Two things that helped:", "What I'm doing differently:")

## Structure

- Open with what happened or what you need (job, intro, ask).
- One paragraph of context max before the ask.
- Close with a direct ask or stop; no fortune-cookie line.

## De-slop priority

1. Remove fake citations and invented company names
2. Cut engagement theater and hashtag walls
3. Collapse lesson lists into plain sentences or delete
4. Keep one concrete detail (place, number, tool, date)

## After linktwerk

When input came from `linktwerk`, assume **score 0–1** even if buzzword scan passes. Full de-slop required.
