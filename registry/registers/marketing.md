# Marketing register

**When:** taglines, hero copy, feature blurbs, landing-page text (plain text or extracted from components).

## Voice

- Specific claims over vague uplift.
- Active verbs with objects ("Syncs invoices to QuickBooks", not "Streamlines your workflow").

## Constraints

- **No em dashes.** No colon runways ("The result:").
- Every claim falsifiable or clearly scoped ("Up to 40% faster on 10k-row CSVs", not "Revolutionary speed").
- No buzzword stack (world-class, seamless, cutting-edge in one paragraph).
- No faux frameworks or letter acronyms as filler.
- If the target is `.tsx`/`.html` with layout, route visual slop to `$impeccable`; fix copy only here.

## Structure

- Headline: one concrete benefit or contrast.
- Subhead: how, for whom, or constraint — not a restatement of the headline.
- Feature bullets: verb + object + outcome; vary grammar across bullets.

## De-slop priority

1. Replace abstract verbs (leverage, unlock, empower) with what the product literally does
2. Cut hero intro that repeats the product name
3. Remove numbered eyebrows (01 / 02 / 03) unless showing a real sequence
4. One proof point per section (metric, customer, constraint) where possible
