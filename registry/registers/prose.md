# Prose register

**When:** emails, essays, personal narratives, letters, social posts (non-cringe).

## Voice

- Sounds like one person talking to one reader.
- Contractions OK when the author used them.
- First person OK when the draft uses it.

## Constraints

- **No isn't-it's pivots** ("The annoying part isn't… It's…").
- No lesson-list takeaways after a personal story.
- No performative wit ("felt on brand", staccato "No X, just Y" openers).
- No keynote cadence (aphoristic pairs, manufactured reveals).
- No engagement bait closers ("Thoughts?", "Agree?").
- No meta-AI voice ("I hope this helps", "Certainly!").
- Specific details beat abstract lessons. Names, dates, places, numbers when available.

## Structure

- Open with the point or the scene, not throat-clearing.
- One idea per paragraph; vary sentence length.
- Close when done; no "In conclusion" scaffold.

## De-slop priority

1. Cut opener/closer scaffolding
2. Replace vague uplift with concrete detail
3. Break uniform triplet rhythm
4. Preserve the author's tone (formal stays formal; casual stays casual)
